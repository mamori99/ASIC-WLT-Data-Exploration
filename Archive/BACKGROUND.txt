In general BIN=1 is for parts passing all test in the specific temperature (CP2, CP3, CP4). If a part is failing in a temperature my be still measured in other temperature and will have hard_bin = 20 as binning. The hard_bin number is usually related to the failing test (i.e. hard_bin=17 for noise fails, since the noise test are called T17_ ). The soft_bin is instead more specific on the failing test (i.e. accel noise, gyro noise ecc.)
right now the target accel noise test is the T17_62 in the variant F3 (T17_62 F0 and T17_65 F5/F7 are additional noise test with different capacitances). It is the one correlating best with FT and lab measurement (there may still be some interesting correlation over temperature with the other tests)
ACCGMN is our high performance mode (HPM), ACCALPN is the NORM mode (HPM is of higher interest at the moment)
